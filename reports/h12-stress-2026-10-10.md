# H12 stress test 2026-10-10

Paper only. Spot DLMM inventory. No perps, no borrowed leverage, no lending. Gamma is the Cartea risk-aversion constant, not borrowed capital. Deposit $250 unless a row says otherwise. SOL $110.41. Replay is the crossed-bin fee model plus exit slippage, transaction fees, and non-refundable bin-array rent. A 1× cost row matches that model. 1.5× and 2× scale slippage and transaction fees. Rent is unchanged.

## Sample

Discovered pools: 892. Pools with at least eight candles on some free timeframe: 881. Daily bars are a 90-day Meteora window. 4h bars are 16 days. 1h bars are 48 hours; a 7-day 1h request is rejected by the API.

The walk-forward uses daily bars. Lookback 14 days, hold 7 days, step 2 days. 12150 entries, 279 distinct coins, 1777 holdout-coin entries. Train is the first 70% of train-coin calendar days. Test is the rest of those coins. Holdout coins are the sorted 30% sample-key split and only their post-cut entries are kept.

Trials overlap by five days, so the trial count is larger than the number of independent coins. Means below that say coin-equal weight each coin once. The bootstrap resamples coins. Fresh pools are under a day old, so they do not have a 21-day daily history. A high-vol label needs a 25% day or turnover of 8. Pools whose 24h change was not filled can land in older-memecoin instead.

## Look-ahead

Feature windows that contain a bar at or after entry: 0. Gamma and the fee quintile for the primary book are computed from the lookback only. The fixed width is the train argmax. Test and holdout outcomes were not an input to that choice.

Meteora volume history for this window has timestamp, volume, fees, and protocol fees. It has no TVL. Fee/TVL therefore divides pre-entry fees by the scan-time TVL. That denominator was not known at entry. The replacement rank uses pre-entry fees/volume over the same price change, which is known at entry.

Fee/volume quintile, entered out-of-sample: $31.58 (trials 870, coins 163). Selected entries 2456 versus 2422 for fee/TVL. Jaccard overlap 0.34.

If the quintile is taken from the forward window instead of the lookback, entered out-of-sample coin-equal net is $296.97 (selected 2426). If width is also taken from the forward window, it is $312.38. Liquidity depth in the fee model uses the lookback's absolute price change, floored at 10%, not the live 24h print.

892 pools had no 24h price change on the pool payload. 150 of those were filled from DexScreener before classification. Cache size 33815 replays.
