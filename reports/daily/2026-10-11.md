## Leading cell, after costs

Each figure is a $250 paper deposit. Gross is inventory mark plus claimed fees, before costs. Net subtracts exit-swap slippage, signature and priority fees, and non-refundable bin-array rent. Position-account rent is refundable on close and is not in the loss. Impermanent loss is the inventory mark versus holding the tokens the position started with. Versus cash is the net PnL. Versus the token is equity after costs minus buying the volatile asset at the window's first open and holding it to the last close. That token hold is defined only when token X is volatile and token Y is a quote. If both legs are quotes, or the volatile asset is Y, the token hold is the deposit and is not a real token bet.

Drawdown mean, 5th percentile, and max are the distribution of each trial's own max drawdown, not a combined portfolio path. Forward rows use positions opened before this run. Their 5th percentile and worst figure are returns, not path drawdowns. New opens from today are left out so a zero mark does not dilute the book.
