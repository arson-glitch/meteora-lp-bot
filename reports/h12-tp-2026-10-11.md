# H12 take-profit variants 2026-10-11

Paper only. Spot DLMM inventory. No perps, no borrowed leverage, no lending. Deposit $250. SOL $111.53.

These variants keep the pre-registered H12 rule: γ=0.001, top 20% of that day's train-coin fee/TVL over absolute price change, two-sided Spot, 96-hour out-of-range timeout, 18% stop. The stop is still mark-to-market. The only added exit is a take-profit at 10%, 15%, 25%, or 40% of the deposit, measured on position value after exit slippage, transaction fees, and non-refundable bin-array rent. On that exit every non-SOL balance, including unclaimed fees in that token, is swapped to SOL. A SOL balance is not swapped. When neither leg is SOL, the pool walk sells the non-quote inventory and the quote inventory is then sold to SOL at 4 bps against a $10M reserve. On a SOL pair the swap matches the stress-test liquidation. The no-take-profit row uses that same all-non-SOL exit cost so the comparison is the exit, not a second cost model.

Walk-forward matches the stress test. Daily bars, lookback 14 days, hold 7 days, step 2 days. 11678 entries, 269 coins. Train is the first 70% of train-coin calendar days. Test is the rest of those coins. Holdout coins are the sorted 30% sample-key split. The quintile uses train coins only. Width uses the lookback only. Selected entries 2363. Means are coin-equal. Trials overlap, so the bootstrap resamples coins. Versus cash is coin-equal net after costs. Versus hold is that equity minus buying the volatile token. The candle cache is the one on disk for this run, including daily bars refreshed after the 2026-10-10 stress print, so the no-take-profit row is the control for this sample rather than a reprint of that earlier dollar figure.

Promotion gates are unchanged: 8 train / 5 test / 4 holdout coins, beat cash and beat holding on the holdout, no coin above 50% of positive PnL, Bid-Ask excluded. Nothing here is promoted. Nothing is written to strategies/winners. The original H12 trial file and its hash are unchanged.
